# NNNN. Title in the imperative or as the decision itself

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | deprecated | superseded by `NNNN-slug.md`
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD (the date the decision was made, not the date this file was written)
- **Deciders:** roles, not names or emails (optional)

## Context

What forces are at play: the problem, the constraints, what was measured. Link incidents and
investigations rather than retelling them. State facts with dates; mark anything unverified.

## Decision

What we do, stated so a reader can check whether a change complies. One decision per ADR.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we now have to keep doing, and the known debt the
decision leaves behind. Include how to tell if the decision has stopped holding.

## Alternatives considered

Each alternative with the concrete reason it lost. "Not considered" is acceptable if true.

## Links

- PRs: `#NN`
- Incidents: `../incidents/YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`
- Runbooks: `../runbooks/<name>.md`
- Code: repo-relative paths
